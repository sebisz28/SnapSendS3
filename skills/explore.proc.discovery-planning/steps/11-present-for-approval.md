# Step 11: Present for Approval

## Objective

Present the comprehensive Explore Bundle to the human for review and approval.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Steps 1-10 complete with all sections documented

## Actions

### 11.1 Present Bundle Summary

Present for human review:

```
Explore Bundle Complete

Project: [Name]
Timeline: [N] weeks
Phases: [N] phases with [N] total activities

Summary:
- Open Questions: [N] questions across [N] categories
- Active Assumptions: [N] assumptions ([X] stakeholder, [Y] technical, [Z] operational, [W] business)
- Risks: [N] risks identified ([X] high, [Y] medium, [Z] low priority)
- Expected Outputs: [N] artifacts
- Constraints: [N] constraint types documented
- Checkpoints: [N] milestone checkpoints

Activity Breakdown:
- Phase 1: [N] activities (Week 1)
- Phase 2: [N] activities (Weeks 2-3)
- Phase 3: [N] activities (Week 4)

Review:
- Approve (bundle is complete, start discovery)
- Adjust scope (tell me what to add/remove)
- Clarify activity (tell me which activity needs detail)
- Add checkpoint (tell me what validation point is missing)
- Revise timeline (tell me if duration needs adjustment)
```

**STOP**: Wait for human approval before writing bundle.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Bundle summary presented to human
- [ ] Human approved bundle (or revisions applied)
