# Step 7: Identify Initial Risks

## Objective

Document known risks from the Signal with impact/likelihood assessment and mitigation strategies.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 6 (Active Assumptions) complete with all assumptions documented

## Actions

### 7.1 Document Risks

Create risk inventory:

```
Risks

| Code | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------|--------|-----------|------------|
| R1 | [Risk description] | High/Medium/Low | High/Medium/Low | [Mitigation strategy - reference A# codes where applicable] |
| R2 | [Risk description] | High/Medium/Low | High/Medium/Low | [Mitigation strategy] |
| R3 | [Risk description] | High/Medium/Low | High/Medium/Low | [Mitigation strategy] |

Impact Levels:
- High = Project failure, major delays (>1 month), significant cost overrun (>20%)
- Medium = Moderate delays (1-4 weeks), scope reduction, cost increase (10-20%)
- Low = Minor delays (<1 week), workarounds available, minimal cost impact (<10%)

Likelihood Levels:
- High = >60% probability, known issues, no clear mitigation
- Medium = 30-60% probability, some uncertainty, mitigation possible
- Low = <30% probability, unlikely scenario, well-controlled

Note: Full risk register with detailed mitigation strategies will be created in A10 (Phase 2).
```

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All known risks from Signal documented
- [ ] Each risk has impact and likelihood assessment
- [ ] Mitigation strategies defined (referencing A# codes where applicable)
