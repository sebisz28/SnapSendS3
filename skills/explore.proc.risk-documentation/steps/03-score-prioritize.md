# Step 3: Score and Prioritize

## Objective

Calculate risk scores using impact × likelihood matrix and prioritize risks.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 2 (Document Risks) complete

## Actions

### 3.1 Apply Impact Levels

| Level | Score | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Critical** | 5 | Project failure, >3 month delay, >50% cost overrun |
| **High** | 4 | Major delay (1-3 months), 20-50% cost overrun |
| **Medium** | 3 | Moderate delay (2-4 weeks), 10-20% cost increase |
| **Low** | 2 | Minor delay (<2 weeks), <10% cost impact |

### 3.2 Apply Likelihood Levels

| Level | Score | Probability |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Very High** | 5 | >80%, almost certain |
| **High** | 4 | 60-80%, likely |
| **Medium** | 3 | 30-60%, may occur |
| **Low** | 2 | 10-30%, unlikely |
| **Very Low** | 1 | <10%, rare |

### 3.3 Calculate Risk Score

Risk Score = Impact (1-5) × Likelihood (1-5)

| Score Range | Risk Level | Action Required |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 20-25 | Critical | Immediate action, escalate to leadership |
| 15-19 | High | Urgent mitigation, weekly monitoring |
| 8-14 | Medium | Active mitigation, bi-weekly monitoring |
| 4-7 | Low | Monitor, contingency plan ready |
| 1-3 | Very Low | Accept, periodic review |

### 3.4 Prioritize

Order risks by score, highest first. Identify top 3 high-priority risks.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All risks scored with impact × likelihood
- [ ] Risk scores calculated correctly
- [ ] Risks prioritized by score
- [ ] Top 3 high-priority risks identified
