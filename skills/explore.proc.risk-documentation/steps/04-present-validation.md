# Step 4: Present Risks for Validation

## Objective

Present the risk register summary to the human for review and approval.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 3 (Score and Prioritize) complete

## Actions

### 4.1 Present Risk Register Summary

```
Risk Register Summary

Total Risks: [N]
- Critical: [N] risks
- High Priority: [N] risks
- Medium Priority: [N] risks
- Low Priority: [N] risks

Top 3 High-Priority Risks:

1. [ID]: [Risk title]
   - Impact: [level] - [consequence]
   - Likelihood: [level]
   - Score: [N]
   - Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Owner: [name]

2. [ID]: [Risk title]
   - Impact: [level] - [consequence]
   - Likelihood: [level]
   - Score: [N]
   - Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Owner: [name]

3. [ID]: [Risk title]
   - Impact: [level] - [consequence]
   - Likelihood: [level]
   - Score: [N]
   - Mitigation: [strategy]
   - Owner: [name]

Review:
- Approve (risk register is complete)
- Add risk (tell me what I missed)
- Adjust scoring (tell me which risk to re-score)
- Accept risk (tell me which risk to mark as accepted with rationale)
```

**STOP**: Wait for human validation.

### 4.2 Apply Changes (if needed)

If human requests changes, update risks and re-present.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Risk register presented to human
- [ ] Human has approved or provided feedback
- [ ] Any requested changes applied
