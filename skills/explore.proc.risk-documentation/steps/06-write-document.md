# Step 6: Write Risk Register

## Objective

Write the complete risk register to `explore/explore-[slug]/risks.md`.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 5 (Accepted Risks) complete

## Actions

### 6.1 Write Document

Write complete risk register to `explore/explore-[slug]/risks.md`.

**Use template**: `templates/risk-register-template.md`

**Required sections**:
1. Risk Summary (counts by category and priority)
2. Risk Assessment Matrix (impact/likelihood definitions, score calculation)
3. Technical Risks (T1, T2, ...)
4. Business Risks (B1, B2, ...)
5. Operational Risks (O1, O2, ...)
6. Compliance Risks (C1, C2, ...)
7. Risk Dependencies (interaction map, cascading risks)
8. Mitigation Timeline
9. Risk Monitoring (review schedule, KPIs)
10. Retired Risks (initially empty)
11. Lessons Learned
12. Related Artifacts
13. Enrichment Log

### 6.2 Verify Document

Confirm all sections present with no placeholder text.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Document written to `explore/explore-[slug]/risks.md`
- [ ] All risk categories populated
- [ ] Risk dependencies mapped
- [ ] Mitigation timeline created
- [ ] No placeholder text remaining
