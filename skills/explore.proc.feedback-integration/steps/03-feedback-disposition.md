# Step 3: Feedback Disposition

For each stakeholder change or critique point, recommend a disposition with rationale.

```
Feedback Disposition Table

| # | Feedback Item | Disposition | Rationale | Evidence |
|---|--------------|------------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | [Change/critique] | Accept | [Aligns with architecture, improves design] | [VALIDATED] |
| 2 | [Change/critique] | Accept w/ Modification | [Valid concern, different implementation] | [VALIDATED] |
| 3 | [Change/critique] | Reject | [Contradicts truth hierarchy / creates regression] | [VALIDATED] |
| 4 | [Change/critique] | Defer to LLD | [Valid detail, belongs in lower-level design] | [VALIDATED] |

Summary:
Accept: [N] | Accept w/ Modification: [N] | Reject: [N] | Defer to LLD: [N]

Rejected items — detailed rationale:
| # | Feedback Item | Rejection Reason | Architectural Rule Violated |
|---|--------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| [N] | [Item] | [Why rejected] | [Which rule or decision it conflicts with] |

How would you like to proceed?
- All approved — integrate accepted feedback into revised draft
- Override dispositions — tell me which items and new disposition
- More conservative — reject additional items (tell me which)
- More permissive — accept additional items (tell me which)
```

**STOP**: Wait for architect to approve or override the disposition table.
