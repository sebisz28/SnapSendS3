# Step 8: Generate Specification Completion (Group 4)

## Objective

Generate Group 4 sections: Open Questions, Dependencies, Assumptions, Epic Extraction plan, Implementation Notes, and Approval & Sign-Off.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 7 (Review Group 3) complete with human approval

## Actions

### 8.1 Generate Group 4 Sections

- **Open Questions**: Every `[ASSUMPTION]` from Signal and discovery → prioritised open question with owner and target resolution date.
- **Dependencies**: Upstream (must complete before), downstream (blocked by this), external (third-party).
- **Assumptions**: Documented assumptions that may affect implementation — table: # · Assumption · Risk if Wrong · Validation Plan.
- **Epic Extraction**: Plan for breaking PRD into epics. Map requirements (`R-XXX`) to proposed epics.
- **Implementation Notes**: Phasing, technical considerations, design considerations.
- **Approval & Sign-Off**: 4-role approval table (Product Manager, Tech Lead, UX Lead, Stakeholder).

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All assumptions converted to open questions
- [ ] Dependencies mapped (upstream, downstream, external)
- [ ] Assumptions documented with risk and validation plan
- [ ] Epic extraction plan outlined
- [ ] Implementation notes added
- [ ] Approval table present
