# Step 7: Assumptions & Gaps Catalogues

## Objective

Document what's assumed vs what's unknown with detailed tracking, including risk assessment and validation plans.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 6 (Stakeholder Map) complete with confirmed stakeholders and RACI

## Actions

### 7.1 Create Assumptions Catalogue

Document assumptions from Signal with validation tracking:

```
Assumptions Catalogue

| # | Assumption (from Signal) | Open Question | Risk if Wrong | Owner | Closes In |
|---|--------------------------|---------------|---------------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | [Assumption statement] | [Question to resolve assumption] | [Impact if incorrect] | [Stakeholder name] | SP2 / A# |
| 2 | [Assumption statement] | [Question to resolve assumption] | [Impact if incorrect] | [Stakeholder name] | SP2 / A# |
```

### 7.2 Create Gaps Catalogue

Document items that SP2 must gather via stakeholder interviews:

```
Gaps Catalogue

Items SP2 must gather (via stakeholder interviews):

| # | Gap | Why It Matters | Question for Stakeholder | Stakeholder |
|---|-----|----------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | [Gap description] | [Impact on project] | [Specific question to ask] | [Stakeholder name] |
| 2 | [Gap description] | [Impact on project] | [Specific question to ask] | [Stakeholder name] |

Note: All assumptions will become tracked in Explore Bundle and resolved through activities.
All gaps will be addressed in SP2 stakeholder interviews.

Confirm or add missing assumptions/gaps.
```

**STOP**: Wait for human to validate assumptions and gaps.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] All assumptions documented with open questions and risk
- [ ] Each assumption has an owner and target resolution phase
- [ ] All gaps documented with why they matter
- [ ] Each gap has a specific question for a named stakeholder
- [ ] Human validated assumptions and gaps
