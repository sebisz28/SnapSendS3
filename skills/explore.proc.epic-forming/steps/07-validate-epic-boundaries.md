# Step 7: Validate Epic Boundaries

## Objective

Ensure epic boundaries remain logical and coherent, maintaining consistency with established patterns and providing clean interfaces for iteration planning.

## Entry Criteria

- Epic content created or amended (Step 5 complete)
- Epic epochs formed and validated (Step 6 complete)
- Epic structure populated with capability information
- Ready for boundary validation and consistency checks

## Actions

### 7.1 Validate Boundary Coherence

Assess epic boundary integrity:
- **Logical Grouping**: Do capabilities in epic make sense together?
- **Boundary Clarity**: Are epic boundaries clear and unambiguous?
- **Scope Appropriateness**: Is epic scope appropriate for planning?
- **Pattern Consistency**: Do boundaries follow established patterns?

### 7.2 Check Cross-Epic Relationships

Validate relationships between epics:
- **Overlap Analysis**: Are there capability overlaps between epics?
- **Gap Analysis**: Are there capability gaps between epics?
- **Dependency Mapping**: How do epics depend on each other?
- **Interface Clarity**: Are epic interfaces well-defined?

### 7.3 Assess Iteration Planning Fit

Evaluate epic as iteration planning interface:
- **Planning Efficiency**: Does epic provide good iteration boundaries?
- **Task Grouping**: Can capabilities be logically grouped for implementation?
- **Stakeholder Clarity**: Does epic make sense to stakeholders?
- **Implementation State**: Does epic clearly communicate what exists vs what needs building?

### 7.4 Identify Boundary Issues

Document and address any boundary problems:
- **Boundary Bleeding**: Capabilities that should be in different epics
- **Scope Creep**: Epic scope that's too broad or unfocused
- **Pattern Violations**: Grouping that doesn't follow established patterns
- **Interface Confusion**: Unclear boundaries between related epics

## Discussion Point (Governed Mode)

**STOP**: Present boundary validation for confirmation:
- "I've validated the epic boundaries and found:"
- "Boundary coherence: [assessment of logical grouping]"
- "Cross-epic relationships: [overlap/gap analysis]"
- "Iteration planning fit: [assessment of planning efficiency]"
- "Any boundary issues: [problems identified and proposed solutions]"
- "Do these boundary validations align with your expectations?"

## Heuristic (Delegated Mode)

If in delegated mode:
- Validate boundary coherence using established pattern criteria
- Check cross-epic relationships systematically
- Assess iteration planning fit using efficiency metrics
- Identify and document boundary issues automatically
- Proceed to Step 8 with validated boundaries

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Epic boundaries validated for logical coherence
- [ ] Cross-epic relationships analyzed and documented
- [ ] Iteration planning fit assessed
- [ ] Boundary issues identified and documented
- [ ] Ready for epic indexing and linking
