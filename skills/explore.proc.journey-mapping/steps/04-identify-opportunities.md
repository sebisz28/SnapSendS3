# Step 4: Identify Opportunities

## Objective

Analyze pain points across all stages and identify improvement opportunities with value, effort, and priority.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 3 (Map Current State) complete for all stages

## Actions

### 4.1 Analyze Pain Points and Propose Opportunities

```
Opportunities Identified

Based on the pain points, I see these improvement opportunities:

Stage [N]: [Stage name]
- Pain: [Pain point description]
- Opportunity: [How to improve]
- Value: [High/Medium/Low]
- Effort: [High/Medium/Low]
- Priority: [High/Medium/Low]

Review these opportunities:
- Approve (opportunities are correct)
- Adjust priority (tell me which should be higher/lower)
- Add opportunity (tell me what I missed)
```

**STOP**: Wait for human validation of opportunities.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Opportunities identified for each significant pain point
- [ ] Each opportunity has value, effort, and priority ratings
- [ ] Human has validated opportunities
