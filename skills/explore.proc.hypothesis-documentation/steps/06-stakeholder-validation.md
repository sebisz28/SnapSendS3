# Step 6: Stakeholder Validation

## Objective

Present hypothesis for stakeholder confirmation with evidence, assumptions, and success criteria.

## Entry Criteria

- [ ] Step 5 (Success Criteria) complete

## Actions

### 6.1 Present Hypothesis for Review

```
Hypothesis Validation Session

Hypothesis: [Statement]

Evidence Strength: [X]% validated, [Y]% assumptions
Confidence: [High/Medium/Low]

Critical Assumptions:
1. [Assumption 1]
2. [Assumption 2]

Success Criteria:
- [Metric 1]: [Target]
- [Metric 2]: [Target]

Stakeholder Review:
- Do you agree with this hypothesis?
- Are the success criteria measurable and realistic?
- Are there critical assumptions we're missing?
- What would cause you to reject this hypothesis?
```

**STOP**: Wait for stakeholder validation.

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] Hypothesis presented to stakeholders
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback recorded
- [ ] Agreement or revision requests captured
